# Gabla Login Asset Prompt

Use this prompt in an image generation model when replacing the local SVG assets with fully illustrated artwork:

Create a cinematic login screen for a premium service platform called "Gabla".

Requirements:
- Background: a beautiful night city view with warm yellow lights and a dark blue sky.
- Centerpiece: a radiant blue bowl at the bottom center, glowing with golden light.
- Icons: eight service icons (plumbing, electrical, carpentry, cleaning, painting, mechanic, welding, more services) should animate one by one coming out from the bowl with a smooth cinematic effect, floating to their positions around the bowl.
- Icon style: use provided gold circular icon chips.
- Login card: dark blue glassmorphism card in the center above the bowl.
- Inputs: email and password with icons.
- Buttons: gold sign in button, white Google sign in button.
- Links: forgot password and sign up.
- Fonts: use Poppins.
- Colors: use a palette based on deep navy, royal blue, warm gold, and soft white.
- Animations:
  - The bowl glows and emits particles.
  - Service icons rise from inside the bowl one by one with golden trails.
  - The login card fades in and slightly floats.
- Keep the design modern, premium, cinematic, and responsive for desktop and mobile.

Suggested palette:
- Gold: `#FFC107`
- Navy: `#0D1B2A`
- Royal blue: `#1E3A8A`
- White: `#E6E6E6`
- Soft gray: `#8A8A8A`
