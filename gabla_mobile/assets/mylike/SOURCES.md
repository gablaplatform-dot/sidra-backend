# Mylike preview references

- Shop and product details follow the two screens in the supplied shop image. The runtime displays its product and offer photo regions. Titles, navigation, choices and controls are Flutter widgets.
- Events and event details follow the supplied event image. The poster and portrait regions come from the supplied image.
- Jobs uses the compact company, role and metadata card hierarchy from [Jobify](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/619385755042076715/).
- Ride uses the map, fare sheet and driver confirmation hierarchy from [Riider](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/1123437069557643328/).
- Bus uses the photo, route form, red action and recent trips hierarchy from [Bus Booking Mobile App UI Template](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/450852612719713225/). The coach photograph is the [Unsplash bus photograph](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1603521801204-8d9c70dd08c8) already referenced by the Gabla demo seed.

Design Studio opens a separate presentation with example data and local interactions. A few supporting pages share form and record layouts. Device fonts, system bars and map imagery affect pixel-level appearance.
